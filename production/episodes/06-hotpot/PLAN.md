## Promise

After this video, the learner can order a split hotpot, choose the soup base, add beef, and ask for dipping sauce.

## 5 words

| # | 词 | Pinyin | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 火锅 | huǒguō | hotpot |
| 2 | 鸳鸯锅 | yuānyāngguō | split pot |
| 3 | 锅底 | guōdǐ | soup base |
| 4 | 牛肉 | niúròu | beef |
| 5 | 蘸料 | zhànliào | dipping sauce |

`辣` 作为 P04 的辅助表达，不占核心词位。

## Sentence

```text
今天吃火锅，我要一个鸳鸯锅，锅底一边辣、一边不辣，再来一盘牛肉和一份蘸料。
Jīntiān chī huǒguō, wǒ yào yí ge yuānyāngguō,
guōdǐ yì biān là, yì biān bú là,
zài lái yì pán niúròu hé yí fèn zhànliào.
Today I’m having hotpot. I’d like a split pot — one spicy side,
one mild side — plus a plate of beef and a portion of dipping sauce.
```

注：`一边辣、一边不辣` 是“这一边 / 另一边”，不是“一边……一边……”同时做两件事的语法点。

- 蘸料是小碗 + 勺，不是饮料。
