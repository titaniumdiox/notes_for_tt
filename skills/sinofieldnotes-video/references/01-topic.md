# 01 · Topic selection

## Constraint

A topic must fit **one scene, one transaction/dialogue, 5 words, 1 sentence, 60s**.  
If it needs a second location, a second character, or a voiceover explanation longer than 60s, reject it.

## Three-candidate rule

Always propose 3 topics. Do not start with 1.

Score each:

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Intent clarity | vague | broad | clear | “I need this exact sentence” |
| Repeatability | one-off | occasional | monthly | weekly/daily |
| Visualizability | abstract | partly | clear | one gesture/object tells it |
| Cultural specificity | generic | mild | clear | uniquely Chinese |
| Level fit | too hard | mixed | mostly HSK1–2 | exactly HSK1–2 |

Total 0–15 (5 dimensions × 0–3). Pick **≥12**. If none hit 12, change the angle, not the words.

## Good topic shape

- “Order a milk tea”
- “Ask for less ice / half sugar”
- “Buy a train ticket to Shanghai”
- “Ask a coworker to lunch”
- “Check into a hotel”
- “Say you’re full at a Chinese dinner”

## Bad topic shape

- “Chinese food culture” (too broad)
- “The history of tea” (no transaction)
- “How Chinese people think about time” (abstract)
- “10 useful phrases” (no scene)
- “A day in Shanghai” (too many locations)

## Topic promise

Write one sentence:

> After this video, the learner can ___ in Chinese.

Examples:

- “After this video, the learner can order a milk tea with half sugar, less ice, and to go.”
- “After this video, the learner can ask for a table for two and say no spicy.”

If the promise needs “and also…”, it is two episodes.

## Deliverable

Fill `templates/episode-brief.md`:

- topic
- scene
- learner level
- 3 candidates + scores
- chosen topic + promise
- 5 words (draft)
- 1 sentence (draft)
- palette direction
